Calendar for Hyprland

A personal desktop calendar client for the Hyprland Wayland compositor.

Calendar for Hyprland shows Google Calendar events in a desktop calendar popup and sends local reminders for upcoming events. It connects to the Google Calendar API with read-only access, runs entirely on your own computer, and transmits your data nowhere except to Google itself.

Built on the open-source Waylandar project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Google or the Waylandar project.
