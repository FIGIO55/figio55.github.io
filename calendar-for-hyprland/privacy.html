Privacy Policy — Calendar for Hyprland

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Calendar for Hyprland is a personal desktop calendar client. It runs entirely on your own device. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a Google Account, the app requests this scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly — read-only access to your calendars and events.

This lets it read the calendar list, event titles, times, descriptions, locations and reminders, plus the account email address used to label the account. The app never creates, edits or deletes anything in Google Calendar.

Where data is stored

  • OAuth credentials and tokens: ~/.config/waylandar/ and ~/.cache/waylandar/ on your computer, with owner-only file permissions (0600).
  • Event data is cached locally in ~/.cache/waylandar/ so the calendar can be shown offline.

No calendar data is sent to the developer or to any third party. Requests go directly from your device to the Google Calendar API. The app contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising.

How data is used

Only to display your events in the desktop calendar and to show local desktop notifications for upcoming events. It is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness decisions or training AI/ML models.

Sharing

Your data is not shared or sold. The only external service contacted is Google's Calendar API itself.

Google Limited Use disclosure

Calendar for Hyprland's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. To remove it, delete ~/.config/waylandar/ and ~/.cache/waylandar/. To revoke the app's access, use the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the app can no longer sync.

Security

Tokens are stored with owner-only permission and transmitted only over HTTPS to Google. You are responsible for the security of your device and account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the “last updated” date will change. Continued use after changes means you accept them.
